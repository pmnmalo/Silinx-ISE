In short:

- **Select whole branch:** a new tick box beside Keep connections. Ticked (the default), a click on a wire selects the
  whole network of wires of its signal (#2228); not ticked, only the straight segment clicked, which Delete removes alone.

In detail:

- **Select whole branch in the schematic editor (#2211):** the choice remembered; with curved wires, a read-only view or
  several wires selected, a wire is selected whole; Copy takes the whole wire.
