In short:
- A QR code in Share Project Copy… and Share Project Live…: students scan the teacher's screen to open the shared project on a phone or another computer; Enlarge shows it as large as the window, for a projector. A full link too long for a QR code (most projects') gets none, the console suggesting the short link, whose QR code Show QR Code makes.

In detail:
- The QR code of a sharing dialog's link (#3637): a small encoder of its own, `core/qr.js` (byte mode, the smallest version that holds the link, the highest error correction that still fits it, the mask of least penalty; no network), tested by decoding its symbols back with jsQR (a development dependency, the tests' only) at every version from 1 to 40 and with each mask. Share Project Copy… draws the link it shows (`web/js/link-qr.js`): the full link's; the short link's once made (Show QR Code makes its gist, which Copy Link then copies, no second one); a link over 2953 bytes, none, said once in the console. Share Project Live… draws its follow link's. Enlarged in a dialog of its own; an image of its SVG (the page's CSP as it was). The MCP's `project_link` and `share_project` take `qr`: the link's QR code as an SVG (`qr`), a full link too long for one a warning. Portuguese labels.
